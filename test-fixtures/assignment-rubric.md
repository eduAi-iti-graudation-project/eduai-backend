# Persuasive Essay — Climate Action

**Course:** English Language Arts — Grade 10
**Due Date:** December 15, 2026
**Total Points:** 100

## Assignment Description

Write a 5-paragraph persuasive essay arguing for a specific climate action policy that your local community should adopt. Your audience is the city council. You must present a clear thesis, support it with at least three distinct arguments backed by evidence, and address one counterargument.

## Grading Rubric

### 1. Thesis & Focus — 20 points
Clear, specific, arguable thesis; every paragraph ties back to it

### 2. Evidence & Research — 25 points
Compelling evidence from 3+ credible sources; seamlessly integrated

### 3. Counterargument & Rebuttal — 15 points
Fairly presented counterargument with strong, logical rebuttal

### 4. Organization & Structure — 20 points
Logical flow; effective transitions; clear intro, body, conclusion

### 5. Conventions & Style — 20 points
Virtually no grammar errors; sophisticated sentence variety; proper MLA
